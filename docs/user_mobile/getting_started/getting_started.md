# Getting started on mobile

On iOS and Android, a loading screen shows the current startup step, such as
preparing storage or restoring documents. The bar advances as startup phases
finish; it is not a download percentage or time estimate. Your workspace appears
when initialization is complete. If startup cannot finish, close and reopen the
app as the loading screen instructs.

Logistics brings files, readers and chat into one mobile workspace. The available
sources and actions depend on your device and configured connections.

## Find your way around

Use the main navigation to open documents, browse files, visit Workspaces or open
Settings. Open documents stay in the workspace when you change destinations.
Tap a document to return to it. Close a document when you no longer need its open
view; closing a saved book does not delete its retained copy.

Workspaces brings configured folders and remote connections together. Start with
a local file to try the readers before configuring a remote connection.

## Try a document before configuring a connection

Use a disposable text or Markdown file for your first exploration. Open it from
a local source or share a copy from another app, then find its open view in
Documents. Switch to another destination and return to the document. Its open
view should remain available until you close it.

If you edit it, save and reopen it to check the result. A file received from
another app is a copy; saving it does not write back to that app's original.
For creating a document and choosing its destination, follow
[document workflows](../documents/workflows.md). For remote, offline or encrypted
copies, read [remote and offline files](../documents/remote.md) before transferring
or exporting private content.

## Make the app comfortable

Documents always uses compact rows without text-preview cards. Under Settings →
Text & Markdown, choose an editor and preview font size.
Books and comics have their own Appearance controls inside their readers.

Android also offers High contrast / E Ink. Its switch applies immediately and
persists on this device. Turn it off to return to the normal device-following
appearance. Images keep their original colors.
